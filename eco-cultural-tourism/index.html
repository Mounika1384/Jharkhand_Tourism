Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your trip

What is the best time to visit Jharkhand?
The ideal time to visit is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and comfortable for sightseeing.
Are the wildlife sanctuaries safe?
Yes, they are completely safe. Always follow the guidelines provided by your tour guide or forest officials.
How can I book a guided tour?
You can use the 'Booking' wizard on our platform to request a personalized tour plan, and our experts will arrange it for you.
